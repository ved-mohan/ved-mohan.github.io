A Fair OR–ML Framework for Resource Substitution in Large-Scale Networks

Ved Mohan, El Mehdi Er Raqabi, Pascal Van Hentenryck · Under revision at Transportation Science

Freight imbalances leave equipment in the wrong places. We combine optimization and machine learning to recommend equipment swaps on trips that are already scheduled, balancing network-wide efficiency against the workload in each zone. In our experiments, the approach cut model size by 80% and execution time by 90% while preserving optimality.

Democratizing Large-Scale Re-Optimization with LLM-Guided Model Patches

Tinghan Ye, Arnaud Deza, Ved Mohan, El Mehdi Er Raqabi, Pascal Van Hentenryck · NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on ML×OR

When operating rules change, planners need to update deployed optimization models. Our framework turns plain-language requests into auditable model patches, picks a re-optimization method, and re-solves. We evaluate it on online supply-chain re-optimization and offline university exam scheduling.
